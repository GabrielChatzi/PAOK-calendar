The '''2018–19 season''' was '''[[PAOK FC|PAOK Football Club's]]''' 93rd in existence and the club's [[Super League Greece#Seasons in Alpha Ethniki and Super League Greece|60th consecutive season]] in the top flight of Greek football. The team won the [[2018–19 Super League Greece|Super League]] [[List of unbeaten football club seasons|unbeaten]], defended their [[Greek Football Cup]] title won in 2018, and also competed in the [[UEFA Champions League]] and [[UEFA Europa League]].
