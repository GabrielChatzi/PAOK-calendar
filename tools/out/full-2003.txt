The '''2003–04 season''' was '''[[PAOK FC|PAOK Football Club's]]''' 78th in existence and the club's [[Super League Greece#Seasons in Alpha Ethniki and Super League Greece|45th consecutive season]] in the top flight of Greek football. The team will enter the [[Greek Football Cup]] in the First round and will also enter in [[2003–04 UEFA Cup|UEFA Cup]]  starting from the First round.
