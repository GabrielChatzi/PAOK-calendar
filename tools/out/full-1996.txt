The '''1996–97 season''' was '''[[PAOK FC|PAOK Football Club's]]''' 70th in existence and the club's [[Super League Greece#Seasons in Alpha Ethniki and Super League Greece|38th consecutive season]] in the top flight of Greek football. The team entered the [[Greek Football Cup]] in third round.
