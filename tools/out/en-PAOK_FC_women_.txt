'''Football Club P.A.O.K. Thessaloniki Women's Football''' or with its official name '''FC PAOK Thessaloniki''',<ref>[https://www.uefa.com/womenschampionsleague/history/clubs/81221--paok/ uefa.com] Profile</ref> represents the major [[Greece|Greek]] multi-sports club [[P.A.O.K.|AC PAOK]]<ref>[https://www.uefa.com/MultimediaFiles/Download/competitions/General/01/96/56/86/1965686_DOWNLOAD.pdf uefa.com] 2013/14 entries and coefficients</ref> in the national [[Greek football women A Division|A Division]] and international [[women's association football|women's football]] competitions.

It was founded in 2001 as PAOK took over Olympiada'96 Thessaloniki, which faced economic problems. It is currently the leading women's football team in Greece,<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.womensoccer.gr/History/egxories.htm |title=Womenfootball.gr |access-date=12 October 2011 |archive-date=3 March 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180303050432/http://www.womensoccer.gr/History/egxories.htm |url-status=dead }}</ref> having represented the country in the [[UEFA Women's Champions League]] for the last seven years.<ref>[https://www.uefa.com/womenschampionsleague/history/clubs/81221--paok/]</ref>
