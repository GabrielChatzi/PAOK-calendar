The '''1972–73 season''' was '''[[PAOK FC|PAOK Football Club's]]''' 47th in existence and the club's [[Super League Greece#Seasons in Alpha Ethniki and Super League Greece|14th consecutive season]] in the top flight of Greek football. The team entered the [[Greek Football Cup]] in first round, and also faced [[SK Rapid Wien|Rapid Wien]] in the [[1972–73 UEFA Cup Winners' Cup|UEFA Cup Winners' Cup]] first round.
