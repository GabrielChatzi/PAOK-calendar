The '''1985–86 season''' was '''[[PAOK FC|PAOK Football Club's]]''' 59th in existence and the club's [[Super League Greece#Seasons in Alpha Ethniki and Super League Greece|27th consecutive season]] in the top flight of Greek football. The team entered the [[Greek Football Cup]] in first round and also participated in the [[1985–86 European Cup|European Cup]].
