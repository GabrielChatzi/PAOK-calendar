The '''2024–25 PAOK FC season''' was the club's 99th season in existence and the club's [[Super League Greece#Seasons in Alpha Ethniki and Super League Greece|66th consecutive season]] in the top flight of [[Football in Greece|Greek football]]. In addition to the [[2024–25 Super League Greece|domestic league]], [[PAOK FC|PAOK]] participated in this season's editions of the [[2024–25 Greek Football Cup|Greek Cup]], the [[2024–25 UEFA Champions League|Champions League]], and the [[2024–25 UEFA Europa League|Europa League]]. The season covers the period from 23 July 2024 to 30 June 2025.
