The '''2010–11 season''' was '''[[PAOK FC|PAOK Football Club's]]''' 85th in existence and the club's [[Super League Greece#Seasons in Alpha Ethniki and Super League Greece|52nd consecutive season]] in the top flight of Greek football. The team entered the [[Greek Football Cup]] and also competed in the [[UEFA Champions League]] starting from the [[2010–11 UEFA Champions League#Third qualifying round|third qualifying round]].
